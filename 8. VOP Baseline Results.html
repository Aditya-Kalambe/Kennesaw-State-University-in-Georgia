Voice of the Process (VOP) – Baseline Results

Key Baseline Measures

Measure Baseline Result
Average checkout waiting time 8.5 minutes
Median checkout waiting time 8.0 minutes
Percent of customers waiting > 7 minutes 35%
Abandoned orders during rush (per 2-hour window) About 3–5 observed per morning
Average queue length at 8:30 a.m. 12–15 people

Baseline Takeaways

  • The process clearly does not meet the informal “under 7 minutes” expectation.
  • Variation is high; some students are served quickly, while others wait much longer.
  • There is enough pain (both customer and staff) to justify deeper analysis in the next phase.
