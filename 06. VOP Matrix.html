Voice of the Process (VOP) Matrix – Checkout Process

This matrix links process steps to what the process is actually delivering today (wait times, error rates) so we can compare later to what customers want.

Process Step Key Output Measure How It Behaves Today (Baseline) Why It Matters
Join Queue Queue length (people) Often 10–15 people during peak; max observed 22. Long lines scare off some students.
Wait in Line Checkout waiting time (minutes) Average ~8.5 min; 35% > 7 min. Core VOP signal that process is too slow.
Place Order Order entry time (seconds) 45–60 seconds, longer for complex drinks. Slower entry increases total wait.
Payment Payment time (seconds) Cash ~30 sec; card often 45–60 sec, sometimes more. Card payment delays drive peaks in waiting time.
Move to Pickup / Receive Drink Time from payment to drink ready Not deeply measured in this phase; rough estimate 3–5 minutes. Important for overall experience, but secondary in this project’s scope.
