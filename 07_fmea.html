FMEA for the Support Call Process

Purpose

FMEA is a structured way to list how each step in a process can fail, what the effect would be, and how likely and detectable that failure is. We then calculate a Risk Priority Number (RPN) to focus on the most important items. [web:594][web:597][web:598]

Simple FMEA Table

Process Step Failure Mode Effect Cause S (1–10) O (1–10) D (1–10) RPN = S×O×D
Identify request Agent misunderstands main issue Long call, wrong solution Poor listening, no probing questions 7 4 4 112
Research / Resolve Agent cannot find correct article Customer on hold, possible wrong answer Slow, cluttered knowledge base 8 6 5 240
Transfer Unnecessary transfer Longer call, customer frustration Unclear policy on billing ownership 8 5 5 200
Wrap‑up Inaccurate notes Follow‑up calls, rework Rushed documentation 6 4 6 144

Interpretation

The highest RPNs are for “cannot find correct article” and “unnecessary transfer.” These match the earlier Cause & Effect work and support the decision to focus first on knowledge base improvements and clearer billing policies in the Improve phase.
