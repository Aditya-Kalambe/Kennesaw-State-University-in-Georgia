Operational Definitions – Measure Phase

These definitions are written in plain language so everyone measures things the same way.

Metric Operational Definition How It Is Measured In / Out of Scope Notes
Checkout waiting time Time from when a student joins the back of the line until payment is fully completed. Cashier notes the time when the student enters the line and the time when payment is approved; difference in minutes is recorded. In scope Does not include time waiting for the drink after payment.
Long wait Any checkout waiting time greater than 7 minutes. Flagged when recorded waiting time is more than 7.0 minutes. In scope 7 minutes chosen based on student feedback.
Abandoned order A student leaves the line before reaching the cashier. Staff mark a simple tally whenever they see someone leave the line without ordering. In scope Only counted if clearly visible to staff.
Active cashier count Number of cashiers actively serving customers during the 8–10 a.m. window. Supervisor notes how many registers are open at the start of each 15-minute block. In scope Used to see effect of staffing on wait times.
Payment method Whether the customer pays with cash or card. Cashier ticks “cash” or “card” on the timing sheet. In scope Helps compare wait times by payment method.
