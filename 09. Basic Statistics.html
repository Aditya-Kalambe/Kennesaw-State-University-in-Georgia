Basic Statistics – Checkout Waiting Time

Data Set Summary

  • Number of observations: 200 customers
  • Time window: Weekdays, 8–10 a.m., across 2 weeks
Statistic Value (minutes)
Minimum 3.0
25th percentile (Q1) 7.0
Median 8.0
75th percentile (Q3) 10.0
Maximum 15.0
Mean (average) 8.5
Standard deviation 2.3

Plain-Language Interpretation

  • Most students wait between about 7 and 10 minutes to pay.
  • Some unlucky students wait 12–15 minutes, which matches the complaints.
  • The spread (standard deviation) is fairly large, so the experience is not very consistent.
