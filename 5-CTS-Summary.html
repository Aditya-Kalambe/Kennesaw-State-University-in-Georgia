CRITICAL-TO-SATISFACTION (CTS) SUMMARY

Order Processing Efficiency Improvement Project

1. VOICE OF CUSTOMER (VOC) COLLECTION

Method: Conducted 5 customer interviews and 12 web-based surveys (November 2025)

Customer Sample:

  • 3 repeat customers (annual revenue: $50K-$150K each)
  • 2 new customers (testing service)
  • 12 survey respondents (online customers, random sample)

Response Rate: 95% response rate on surveys (12 of 12 sent)

2. VOICE OF CUSTOMER - RAW FEEDBACK

# Customer Quote (VOC) Frequency in Interviews Importance Rating
1 "I need my orders delivered within 2 days - your current 4+ days is a problem" 5 of 5 customers Critical (100%)
2 "Orders need to be correct - I shouldn't have to reorder missing items" 5 of 5 customers Critical (100%)
3 "I want to be able to track my order in real-time" 4 of 5 customers High (80%)
4 "Returns are a hassle - I want to return items easily without problems" 3 of 5 customers High (60%)

3. CTS TRANSLATION - VOC TO INTERNAL REQUIREMENTS

4. CTS PRIORITIZATION

Priority 1 (Must Have - Project Must Achieve):

CTS-1: Cycle Time ≤ 2 days (95% of orders) - Most frequently mentioned, highest impact on customer satisfaction
CTS-2: Accuracy ≥ 99.5% - Equally critical, direct customer impact

Priority 2 (Should Have - Project Should Achieve):

CTS-3: Tracking Accuracy ≥ 99% - Supports cycle time improvement, improves customer confidence
CTS-5: Inventory Accuracy ≥ 99.8% - Fundamental to achieving cycle time and accuracy targets

Priority 3 (Nice to Have - Post-Project):

CTS-4: Return Processing ≤ 1 day - Important but secondary; may be separate project

How CTS Translate to Internal Quality Characteristics:

6. CUSTOMER SATISFACTION MEASUREMENT PLAN

Post-Project Customer Satisfaction Survey:

  • Frequency: Monthly after project completion
  • Sample: 20 random order customers per month
  • Questions:
    • Delivery speed meets expectations (1-10 scale)
    • Order arrived complete and correct (yes/no)
    • Able to track order (1-10 scale)
    • Overall satisfaction (1-10 scale)
  • Target: 9.0+ overall satisfaction score (vs. current 6.8)

7. CTS LINKAGE TO PROJECT GOALS

How Project Success is Defined:

  • ✓ CTS-1: 95% of orders processed in ≤ 2 days = Project achieves timeline goal
  • ✓ CTS-2: Accuracy improves to 99.5% = Reduced customer complaints, revenue protection
  • ✓ CTS-3 & CTS-5: Achieved = Supports primary CTS goals, builds customer confidence
  • ✓ Customer satisfaction improves from 6.8 → 9.0+ = Ultimate project success indicator
