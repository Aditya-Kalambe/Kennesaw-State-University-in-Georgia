Data Collection Plan – Measure Phase

Purpose

To collect simple, reliable data on checkout waiting times and main delay causes during the morning rush (8–10 a.m.) at the campus coffee shop.

Metric Data Source Who Collects When / Frequency Sampling Approach Planned Sample Size
Checkout waiting time (minutes) Manual timing sheet at register Cashiers Weekdays, 8–10 a.m., for 2 weeks Every 3rd customer in line ~200 customers
Payment method (cash vs card) Timing sheet tick box Cashiers Same as above Recorded for each timed customer Same 200 customers
Delay reason (if long wait) Short note on timing sheet Cashiers Only when wait > 7 minutes Judgment sample (all long waits) Estimated 60–80 cases
Active cashier count Observation Shift supervisor Every 15 minutes, 8–10 a.m., 2 weeks Full capture ~80 time blocks

Data Recording Method

  • Use a one-page paper timing sheet per shift.
  • Each row = one observed customer.
  • Columns: time joined line, time payment complete, payment method, long-wait reason (if any).
  • Supervisor collects sheets at end of shift and stores them in a simple folder or shared drive.
