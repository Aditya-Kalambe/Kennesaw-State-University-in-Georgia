COMMUNICATION PLANNING WORKSHEET

Order Processing Efficiency Improvement Project

1. COMMUNICATION STRATEGY OVERVIEW

Purpose: Ensure timely, relevant information flow to all stakeholders, maintaining alignment and addressing concerns throughout project.

Key Principles:

  • Transparency: Share both wins and challenges openly
  • Timeliness: Communicate before issues escalate
  • Relevance: Tailor messages to audience (executive vs. warehouse floor)
  • Two-way: Solicit feedback and address concerns
  • Consistency: Use consistent messaging across channels

4. KEY COMMUNICATION MESSAGES BY PHASE

Define Phase Key Messages:

  • "We're solving a real problem that impacts customers and our business"
  • "This is a structured approach with proven results"
  • "Your input and expertise are critical to success"
  • "We will communicate progress transparently and regularly"
  • "No one's job is being eliminated; we're making jobs better"

5. CRISIS COMMUNICATION PROTOCOL

If budget cuts occur:

  • Immediate notification to sponsor and team
  • Escalation to executive steering committee within 48 hours
  • Revised plan provided within 1 week
  • Transparent communication about implications

If team member leaves / unavailable:

  • Notify all stakeholders within 2 business days
  • Identify backup within 3 days
  • Adjust timeline if critical role affected

If major issue / blocker emerges:

  • Issue reported to sponsor immediately (same day)
  • Root cause analysis within 2 days
  • Recovery plan developed within 5 days
  • All stakeholders updated on resolution
