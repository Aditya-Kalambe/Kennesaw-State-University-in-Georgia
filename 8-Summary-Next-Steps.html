5. RISKS & MITIGATION STRATEGIES

Risk 1: Data Quality Issues
Potential Impact: Poor quality baseline data could invalidate entire analysis
Likelihood: Medium (35%)
Mitigation:
• Implement spot-checking of collected data (daily audits)
• Train data collectors thoroughly before Week 4
• Have backup data collection method (manual if IT system fails)
• Statistical validation of baseline data before moving to Analyze phase
Risk 2: IT System Access / Data Delays
Potential Impact: Cannot extract real-time data; project delayed
Likelihood: Medium (25%)
Mitigation:
• Pre-test IT data access during Weeks 1-3
• Have IT identify backup data extraction method
• Escalate to CIO immediately if access blocked
• Establish manual data collection as contingency
Risk 3: Holiday Season Impact on Data Validity
Potential Impact: December-January volume spikes may not represent normal operations
Likelihood: High (70%)
Mitigation:
• Extend baseline collection into February (4 weeks vs. 2 weeks)
• Separately analyze holiday vs. non-holiday periods
• Note seasonal variation in baseline report
• Adjust targets if needed based on seasonality analysis
Risk 4: Staff Resistance to Additional Tracking
Potential Impact: Data collection compliance low; incomplete data
Likelihood: Medium (30%)
Mitigation:
• Explain importance and purpose of data collection to staff
• Emphasize data is for process improvement, NOT individual performance monitoring
• Keep data collection simple and unburdensome
• Recognize and appreciate staff cooperation
