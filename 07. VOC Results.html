Voice of the Customer (VOC) – Measure Phase

How We Collected VOC

  • Short 3-question paper survey for students at checkout (over one week).
  • Quick interviews with 5 regular students after they got their drinks.
  • 15-minute group chat with 4 staff members after closing.

What Students Said

  • About 70% said they are “okay” with waiting up to 5 minutes.
  • More than half said they get annoyed once the wait passes 7 minutes.
  • Some students said they sometimes skip coffee if the line looks too long.
  • Most care more about speed than about getting a receipt or loyalty stamp.

What Staff Said

  • Morning shifts feel very busy, especially when only one cashier is on duty.
  • Custom drinks slow things down because staff must look up special buttons or prices.
  • Card machines sometimes freeze, forcing staff to retry or cancel transactions.

Simple VOC Conclusions

  • Target wait time should be under 7 minutes, ideally close to 5.
  • Students value speed and predictability more than receipts or small extras.
  • Staff want clearer procedures for busy times and simpler register screens.
