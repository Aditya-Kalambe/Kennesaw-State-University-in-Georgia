5. ENGAGEMENT PLAN BY STAKEHOLDER

Executive Sponsor (VP Operations)

  • Current Status: Enthusiastic Support (8)
  • Engagement Method: Monthly executive steering committee meetings
  • Communication Frequency: Monthly (1 hour meetings)
  • Key Messages: Business ROI, timeline adherence, milestone achievements
  • Issues/Escalations: Budget overruns, timeline delays, scope changes

Finance Director (Priority - CONVERT from Skeptical to Supporter)

  • Current Status: Skeptical (4) → Target: Active Support (7)
  • Concern: ROI unproven, "another Six Sigma project"
  • Engagement Method: Bi-weekly financial tracking reports, early wins celebration
  • Communication Frequency: Bi-weekly (15-min touchpoints + monthly detailed review)
  • Key Messages: $1.2M annual benefit, 16:1 ROI, monthly cost tracking, risk mitigation
  • Tactics:
    • Share case studies of similar successful projects
    • Provide detailed financial model with sensitivity analysis
    • Report early wins (even small ones: $5K monthly cost reduction in Measure phase)
    • Involve in monthly steering committee for visibility
  • Success Indicator: Supports project recommendation, allocates additional resources if needed

IT Department (Priority - Increase from Compliant to Active Support)

  • Current Status: Compliant (5) → Target: Active Support (7)
  • Concern: Competing priorities, resource constraints, "not their project"
  • Engagement Method: Dedicated IT project resource with clear role, weekly tech meetings
  • Communication Frequency: Weekly (30-min technical sync)
  • Key Messages: IT autonomy on solutions, IT will be recognized for success, minimal system disruption
  • Tactics:
    • Assign one senior IT person as full project team member
    • Provide IT recognition in project communications
    • Allow IT to propose technical solutions (not dictated)
    • Include IT in improvement benefits (system performance improvement)
  • Success Indicator: Proactively identifies technical opportunities, advocates for project in IT meetings

Warehouse Manager & Staff

  • Current Status: Active to Compliant (5-7) → Maintain/Increase to Willing-Active (6-7)
  • Concern: Fear of change, potential job security concerns, additional work during implementation
  • Engagement Method: Bi-weekly operations meetings, pilot testing, training involvement
  • Communication Frequency: Bi-weekly team meetings + daily shift briefings during implementation
  • Key Messages: Process improvements will make job easier, no job elimination, staff input valued
  • Tactics:
    • Involve in problem identification and solution design
    • Run pilot with volunteer group before full rollout
    • Provide clear training schedule well in advance
    • Address concerns about workload changes honestly
    • Recognize and celebrate improvements publicly
  • Success Indicator: Staff voluntarily participate in pilots, provide constructive feedback, implement changes smoothly

Customer Service & Finance Support

  • Current Status: Active Support (7)
  • Engagement Method: Monthly updates, involvement in benefits measurement
  • Communication Frequency: Monthly meetings
  • Key Messages: Customer satisfaction improvement, complaint reduction, operational efficiency
  • Tactics: Monthly metrics dashboard showing customer impact improvements

IT Resource Conflict IT diverts resources to emergency issues Medium High - Project delay Secure written IT commitment; identify backup IT person; escalate conflicts to CIO immediately
Warehouse Staff Resistance Staff resists changes, implementation slowdown Medium High - Benefits not realized Transparent communication, pilot testing, training, recognition; listen to concerns
Sponsor Attention Loss Sponsor loses focus due to other business priorities Low Medium - De-prioritization Monthly steering meetings with clear agenda; escalation procedure for decision delays
