Process Map – Campus Coffee Shop Checkout

This simple process map shows the main steps from when a student joins the line to when they leave the counter with their drink. It is meant to match what really happens today, not what is written in any policy.

Step # Step Name What Actually Happens Owner Typical Time (sec)
1 Join Queue Student walks to the counter area and joins the end of the line. Customer —
2 Wait in Line Student moves forward as others are served. Sometimes the cashier steps away, and the line stops. Customer / Cashier Variable (can be several minutes)
3 Place Order Cashier greets student, listens to order, and enters items into the register. Custom drinks sometimes require looking up prices. Cashier 45–60
4 Confirm Total Cashier reads total, student checks it, and may ask to add or remove items. Cashier / Customer 15–30
5 Payment Student pays with card or cash. Card payments sometimes take longer due to network delays. Cashier / Customer 30–60
6 Receipt / Loyalty Receipt is printed or declined. If used, loyalty stamp or app scan happens. Cashier / Customer 10–20
7 Move to Pickup Area Student steps aside and moves to the drink pickup area. Customer —
8 Receive Drink Barista calls the order, student picks up the drink and leaves the counter. Barista / Customer —

For this Measure phase, the main focus is on steps 1–5 (from joining the queue to completing payment).
