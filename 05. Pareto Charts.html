Pareto Analysis – Main Causes of Long Checkout Waits

This page summarizes the Pareto analysis of the reasons behind “long waits” (over 7 minutes). You can paste an image of your chart below and keep the simple table as backup.

Summary Table for Pareto Chart

Cause of Long Wait (> 7 min) Count Percent of Total Cumulative Percent
Only one cashier open 34 40% 40%
Price lookup for custom drinks 22 26% 66%
Slow card payment / network delay 15 18% 84%
New staff training 8 9% 93%
Other misc. issues 6 7% 100%

Interpretation (Plain Language)

  • About two-thirds of long waits come from just two causes: only one cashier open and slow price lookups.
  • If the shop can keep two cashiers open during peak times and simplify/customize drink buttons, many long waits should disappear.
  • Card payment delays and new staff training matter, but less than the first two causes.
