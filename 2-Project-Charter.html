SIX SIGMA PROJECT CHARTER

Order Processing Efficiency Improvement Project

1. BUSINESS CASE

Why This Project Is Important:

  • Customer satisfaction declining due to delayed order delivery (currently 4.2 days vs. 2-day target)
  • 18,000 orders annually delivered late, resulting in ~$450,000 in estimated lost revenue
  • Fulfillment accuracy at 92% creates returns processing burden and customer dissatisfaction
  • Competitor analysis shows market leaders achieving 2-day delivery and 99.5%+ accuracy
  • Market opportunity: 15% revenue increase if order-to-delivery reduced to 2 days with 99.5% accuracy

Business Goals for Success:

  • Reduce average order processing cycle time from 4.2 days to 2.0 days (95% of orders)
  • Improve order fulfillment accuracy from 92% to 99.5% (first-pass yield)
  • Decrease customer complaint volume by 60%
  • Achieve 15% revenue increase from improved customer satisfaction and retention
  • Establish repeatable, sustainable process for maintaining these improvements

2. PROBLEM STATEMENT

Current Situation:

Order fulfillment process at our e-commerce distribution center takes an average of 4.2 business days from receipt to shipment. Customers expect delivery within 2 business days. Additionally, 8% of orders contain errors requiring correction or rework, resulting in customer complaints and processing delays.

Impact:

These delays and errors directly reduce customer satisfaction, increase operational costs through rework, and result in estimated annual revenue loss of $450,000 from lost repeat customers and negative reviews.

3. PROJECT SCOPE

In Scope:

  • Order receipt through shipping confirmation (main fulfillment process)
  • Inventory management and order picking accuracy
  • Packing and labeling processes
  • Shipping coordination and handoff
  • Basic returns processing (initial receipt and verification)

Out of Scope:

  • Full returns processing workflow (may be separate Six Sigma project)
  • Customer service center operations
  • Transportation carrier performance (outside our control)
  • Supplier quality issues (handled separately)

Boundaries:

  • Focus: Internal fulfillment center processes only
  • Geography: Main distribution center location
  • Exclusions: Cannot modify customer order system without IT governance approval
