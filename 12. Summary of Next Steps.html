Summary of Next Steps – From Measure to Analyze

Overall Aim

Use what we learned in the Measure phase to find the main root causes of long checkout waits and prepare for simple, low-cost improvements.

Step Action Owner Timing Expected Benefit
1 Review process map and data with full team (cashiers, baristas, supervisor). Black Belt / Project Lead Next week Shared understanding of where the process really slows down.
2 Run a simple root cause session (e.g., 5 Whys or fishbone) on top Pareto causes. Project Lead + Staff Within 2 weeks Clear list of suspected root causes for long waits.
3 Check staffing patterns (why only one cashier sometimes) using schedule and demand data. Shop Supervisor Within 2 weeks Evidence to support opening a second register during peak time.
4 Review register layout and price lookup process for custom drinks. POS Admin + Cashiers Within 3 weeks Ideas to reduce time spent searching for buttons or prices.
5 Check with IT or bank about card payment delays and possible fixes. Supervisor + IT Contact Within 3 weeks Options to speed up or stabilize card transactions.
6 Prepare Analyze-phase plan (which causes to study first, what extra data is needed). Black Belt / Project Lead End of next month Clear, realistic plan for the Analyze phase.

Optional / Extra Tools Planned

  • Fishbone (Ishikawa) diagram to organize possible causes.
  • Simple run charts to see whether small tests change wait times over days.
  • Maybe a quick cost estimate for adding a second cashier compared to extra sales kept.
